## 3) Full description — English (≤ 4000; this is ~2,700)

```
Puregram is a focused, ad-free messaging app for people who want to decide, once and for all, what they will not open again.

Puregram is an independent, unofficial build based on Telegram's open-source client, and it uses the Telegram API. It is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Telegram. "Telegram" is a trademark of its respective owners. Puregram works with the Telegram network and adds one thing: two lists that only you control.

HOW IT WORKS

People always open. Your chats with real people work exactly as you know them, with nothing checked and nothing asked.

Channels, bots and groups start closed. The first time you open one, Puregram asks you a single question: allow it, or block it forever?

- An allow you can withdraw at any time.
- A block is permanent. It cannot be undone - not by you later, not on another device.

That asymmetry is the whole product. You decide while your resolve is clear, and the decision holds when it is not.

WHAT ELSE IS DIFFERENT

- Channel and bot discovery is removed: no global channel or bot search, no Channels or Apps tabs, no similar-channel suggestions, no inline bots, no web image search, no free-text GIF or sticker search. Searching your own chats still works, and your saved stickers, GIFs and emoji are untouched.
- Anything Telegram itself marks as restricted or sensitive is refused, and the "show sensitive content" switch is removed.
- Puregram itself shows you no ads and sells you nothing.

YOUR LISTS FOLLOW YOU

Your lists are stored with your Telegram account, so the same allowed and blocked chats appear on Android and on Windows with nothing to move by hand. A new device has to be approved from a device you already use.

PRIVACY

We store only what the feature needs: your numeric Telegram account id, a device identifier, and the lists you set. We do not read or store your messages, your contacts, or your media - messaging runs entirely over Telegram's network and encryption and never touches our servers.

- No ads, no tracking, no analytics
- Nothing sold, nothing shared
- No extra account or password - just your Telegram login
- Completely free

You can request deletion of everything we store from inside the app. It takes effect after 60 days, and you can cancel during that time. The wait is deliberate: deletion also clears your permanent blocks, and we will not make that a one-tap escape.

Privacy policy: https://puregram.app/privacy.html

AVAILABLE ON

- Android (this listing)
- Windows Desktop (separate download at puregram.app)

Puregram is open-source software based on Telegram's clients; source and license attribution are available at puregram.app.

Note: Puregram is an unofficial, independent application and is not affiliated with Telegram.
```
